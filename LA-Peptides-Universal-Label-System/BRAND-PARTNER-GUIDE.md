# Brand Partner Guide

For onboarding a new white-label brand partner — whether you're the LA Peptides team member setting them up, or a partner reviewing what we need from you. No coding required.

## What we need from a new brand partner

To generate labels for a new partner, we need exactly two things, once:

**1. Your logo** — an icon-style mark (see "Logo guidelines" below), as an `.svg` file if you have one (preferred — it stays sharp at any size), otherwise a high-resolution `.png` or `.jpg`.

**2. Your brand details:**

- Brand name (as it should appear on the label)
- A short subtitle, if you use one (e.g. a division name)
- A short tagline (a few words, e.g. "Precision Wellness")
- Your brand colors — we'll ask for these as hex codes (`#RRGGBB`); if you don't have exact codes, send us your logo and a brand guide/style sheet and we'll match them
- Preferred fonts, if you have brand fonts; otherwise we'll use a clean, professional default

That's the entire one-time setup. Every product you sell afterward reuses this same brand identity automatically — you never redo this step for a new peptide or a new batch.

## Logo guidelines

- **Best**: a simple, icon-style mark (like a lettermark, monogram, or simple graphic symbol) — not a wide wordmark. The logo sits in a small, roughly-square box next to your brand name (which is set separately as text, in your brand font).
- Vector (`.svg`) is strongly preferred — it never pixelates at any print size.
- Your logo will be **automatically scaled to fit its box, preserving its proportions exactly — it will never be stretched or squashed.** If your logo's shape is very different from a square (e.g. a long horizontal wordmark), it will appear smaller within the box to keep its proportions correct, rather than filling every edge.
- Avoid logos with fine detail or thin lines — the box is small (roughly the size of a fingernail on the final printed label), and very fine detail may not be legible at print size.

## What happens next

Once we have your brand details, we build:

1. A **preview image** (`label-preview.png`) showing exactly what your label will look like for a sample product.
2. You review it and tell us if anything needs adjusting (colors, logo size/placement within its box, wording).
3. Once approved, we can generate a label for **any product you sell** just by supplying that product's details (name, strength, lot number, use-by date, and a link to its certificate of analysis) — the layout, your logo, and your colors carry over automatically every time.

## What you'll receive per product/batch

For every product batch, you'll get:

- `label-preview.png` — what the label looks like
- `label-print.pdf` — the exact-size, print-ready file
- (on request) `label.svg` — an editable version, if your own team wants to make further adjustments

## Frequently asked questions

**Can we update our logo or colors later?** Yes — send the updated logo/colors and we rebuild every future batch with the new brand identity. Already-printed batches aren't affected.

**Can different products have different taglines or descriptors?** The brand name, logo, and colors stay consistent across all your products (that's what makes it recognizably "your brand"), but each product's own details (name, strength, descriptor, claims) are set per product.

**What if our logo doesn't fit well in the box?** Tell us — we can adjust how the logo is cropped/centered within its box (its box size and position on the label itself stay fixed, so the design remains consistent across all brand partners), or suggest a simplified version of your mark that will read better at this size.

**Do we need any special software?** No — you'll receive a preview image and a print-ready PDF. If your own team wants to make edits, standard tools (Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Acrobat, or Canva) can open the editable files; see `DEVELOPER-GUIDE.md` for those instructions if you have a technical team.
